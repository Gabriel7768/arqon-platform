ARQON Platform — ADR-005
Architecture Consolidation Review

Reviewer Role

You are acting as an independent Principal Software Architect performing the final architecture consolidation before implementation.

This is NOT another architecture redesign.

This is NOT another architecture critique.

This is the final consistency review before ADR-001 is updated.

Assume the following documents exist:

ADR-001 — Architecture Specification (baseline)
ADR-002 — Runtime & API Review
ADR-003 — Architecture Completeness Review
ADR-004 — Architecture Validation Review

ADR-004 is the authoritative decision regarding which recommendations should be accepted, postponed or rejected.

Your responsibility is to verify that the accepted recommendations can be incorporated into ADR-001 without introducing contradictions or architectural drift.

Do NOT propose new architecture.

Do NOT invent new improvements.

Do NOT search for additional best practices.

Only validate consistency.

Does it change runtime behavior?
Does it affect module boundaries?
Does it require additional documentation?

Review the final public API.

Confirm that:

No unnecessary abstraction exists.
