ARQON Platform — ADR-006
Specification Integrity Review
Status: Final specification audit before implementation
Reviewer role: Independent Principal Software Architect and Specification Auditor
Authoritative document: ADR-001 (already updated with all accepted ADR-004 recommendations)

Mission
Do NOT redesign the architecture.
Do NOT introduce new features.
Do NOT propose future improvements.
Do NOT speculate about enterprise scenarios.

Assume ADR-001 is now the single source of truth.

Your only responsibility is to verify that the specification is internally complete, self-consistent, unambiguous and implementation-ready.

The review must behave like the final audit performed before freezing an architecture specification.

==================================================
OBJECTIVES

Perform a complete integrity review of ADR-001.

• inconsistent terminology
• hidden ambiguity
• conflicting lifecycle descriptions

• runtime inconsistencies

• documentation gaps
