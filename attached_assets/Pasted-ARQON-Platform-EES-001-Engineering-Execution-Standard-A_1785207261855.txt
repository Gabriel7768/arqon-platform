ARQON Platform — EES-001 Engineering Execution Standard

Assume EDS-001 is complete and frozen.

Do not redesign the documentation system.

Instead, define how engineering work is executed across the ARQON Platform.

The objective is to create the Engineering Execution Standard (EES-001), the operational counterpart to EDS-001.

EDS-001 defines how engineering knowledge is documented.

EES-001 must define how engineering work is performed.

The resulting standard must be suitable for a software platform developed by multiple independent engineering teams over the next decade.

Produce a concise set of engineering principles that every contributor must follow.

Final objective

Produce EES-001 as an official engineering standard that complements EDS-001.

EDS-001 governs engineering documentation.

EES-001 governs engineering execution.

The document must be internally consistent, deterministic, scalable, and suitable for long-term platform development.
